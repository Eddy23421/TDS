# TDS Strategies Repository

Welcome to the **TDS Strategies Repository**!

This repository is dedicated exclusively to **Tower Defense Simulator (TDS)** on Roblox. It contains various strategies designed to help players complete different game modes, earn coins, complete modifiers, and achieve other in-game objectives.

All files and scripts in this repository are related to TDS strategies. No unrelated projects or scripts are intended to be hosted here.

## Repository Contents

The repository may include the following types of strategies:

- **Solo Strategies** – Strategies designed to complete game modes entirely on your own.
- **Coin Farming** – Strategies focused on earning coins as efficiently as possible.
- **Modifier Strategies** – Strategies specifically designed for modifier game modes.
- **Difficulty Strategies** – Strategies for completing different difficulty levels.
- **Special Strategies** – Other strategies designed for specific challenges or objectives.

## Tower Requirements

Each strategy may have different tower requirements. Make sure to check the required towers before attempting a strategy.

**Important:**
- Some strategies may require specific Golden Towers.
- Golden Towers are not always interchangeable with their regular versions.
- If a strategy lists a Golden Tower as required or recommended, follow that requirement for the best results.
- Some towers may be optional, depending on the strategy. These will be mentioned where applicable.

Always read the individual strategy instructions before running or attempting it.

## Strategy Information

Where available, individual strategies may include:

- Required towers
- Estimated completion time
- Estimated coins or gems earned
- Timescale compatibility
- Additional requirements or recommendations
- Strategy scripts or execution instructions

Keep in mind that completion times and earnings are estimates. Actual results may vary depending on gameplay conditions, updates, and other factors.

## Important Notes

- Strategies are provided for Tower Defense Simulator only.
- Some strategies may stop working after game updates.
- Always check tower requirements before attempting a strategy.
- Not every strategy is designed for coin farming.
- Timescale compatibility will be specified when relevant.
- Some strategies may require a specific skill tree or tower upgrade.

## Repository Purpose

The main purpose of this repository is to keep TDS strategies organized and easily accessible.

Whether you're looking for solo strategies, efficient farming methods, or ways to complete modifiers, this repository aims to provide a collection of useful strategies in one place.
